# blinkit-data-lens
# 📊 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze Blinkit's sales performance, outlet characteristics, item categories, fat content, and outlet locations.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of key business KPIs and helps identify patterns and trends in sales across different outlet types and locations.

---

## 🏪 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Blinkit's sales data to understand:

- Overall sales performance
- Sales contribution by item type
- Impact of fat content on sales
- Sales performance across different outlet sizes
- Sales distribution across outlet locations
- Outlet establishment trends over the years
- Performance comparison across different outlet types

The dashboard was developed in **Microsoft Power BI** using interactive visuals, KPIs, filters, and a matrix table.

---

## 🎯 Business Requirements

The dashboard was created based on the following business requirements:

### 1. Total Sales by Fat Content
- Analyze the impact of fat content on total sales.
- Compare other KPIs such as Average Sales, Number of Items, and Average Rating.
- **Chart:** Donut Chart

### 2. Total Sales by Item Type
- Identify the performance of different item types based on total sales.
- Compare item categories to understand their contribution to overall revenue.
- **Chart:** Bar Chart

### 3. Fat Content by Outlet for Total Sales
- Compare total sales across different outlets segmented by fat content.
- Analyze the contribution of Low Fat and Regular products.
- **Chart:** Stacked Column Chart

### 4. Total Sales by Outlet Establishment
- Evaluate how outlet establishment year influences sales.
- Identify sales trends across different outlet establishment periods.
- **Chart:** Line Chart

### 5. Sales by Outlet Size
- Analyze the relationship between outlet size and total sales.
- Compare sales generated by Small, Medium, and High-sized outlets.
- **Chart:** Donut/Pie Chart

### 6. Sales by Outlet Location
- Analyze the geographic distribution of sales.
- Compare sales performance across different outlet location tiers.
- **Chart:** Funnel Chart

### 7. All Metrics by Outlet Type
- Provide a comprehensive comparison of key metrics across outlet types.
- Metrics include:
  - Total Sales
  - Average Sales
  - Number of Items
  - Average Rating
  - Item Visibility
- **Chart:** Matrix/Table

## 🔍 Dashboard Insights

Some key observations from the analysis include:

- The dashboard records approximately **$1.20M in total sales**.
- **Regular-fat products** contribute a significant portion of total sales.
- **Fruits and Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are among the highest-performing item categories.
- **Tier 3 outlets** contribute the highest sales among the three outlet location tiers.
- Medium-sized outlets show a strong contribution to overall sales.
- Supermarket-type outlets generate a major share of the total revenue.
- Outlet establishment trends show fluctuations in sales performance across different years.
